id: "status-feedback"
surface: "app"
title: "Expose meaningful asynchronous status"
use_when: "Saving, searching or loading application data"
avoid_when: "Silent changes that leave task outcome ambiguous"
source: "https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html"
verified_at: "2026-10-16"
---

Plan loading, success, empty and error states around the primary task. Use suitable semantic status communication and avoid repeatedly interrupting users for routine updates.
