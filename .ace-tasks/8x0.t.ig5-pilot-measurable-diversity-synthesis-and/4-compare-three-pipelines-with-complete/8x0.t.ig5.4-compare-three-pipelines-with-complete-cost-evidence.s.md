# Compare three pipelines with complete cost evidence

## Behavioral Specification

### User Experience

An operator compares three delivery strategies with the same acceptance contract and complete accounting, including failure, escalation and missing measurements.

### Expected Behavior

- Preregister three fresh tasks before execution: an ordinary application change, a concurrency/lifecycle problem and verification infrastructure. Run three arms per task (nine pipeline runs): three-family discovery -> Sol -> fresh Flash -> Sol review; single GLM Flash discovery -> Sol -> fresh Flash -> Sol review; Astra end-to-end.
- Use one pinned base/contract/environment per task across arms. Fix exact role bindings, common acceptance tests, final evaluation criteria and execution limits before results are visible. Keep source/result access isolated until final evaluation. Randomize arm launch order within a task; use separate test resources or serialize shared-resource measurements.
- All final artifacts receive the same automated acceptance suite and fresh independent anonymized evaluation. Astra's own reasoning is not its independent acceptance review. Record common evaluation work separately and include it in reported totals; pipeline review work remains attributable to its arm.
- Collect actual stage-level usage when exposed: input/output/cached tokens, provider price/currency/date when known, API charges, elapsed work/critical-path time, failed attempts, escalations, human interventions, harness-reported compactions and completed rounds. Include all discovery and synthesis work, not only the final implementer.
- Missing provider/harness metrics are unknown, never zero or inferred from wall time. Subscription allowance observations and API monetary costs are separate, non-additive measures. Do not estimate 4-6x pricing or savings from model names.
- Report quality first: acceptance met, confirmed remaining defects, unsupported assumptions and adversarial results. Then compare cost/time at equal demonstrated acceptance; do not reward a cheaper failed implementation as an equivalent win.
- A/B/C supplies harness calibration and known regressions only, not a fresh independent effectiveness sample. Nine runs are an exploratory pilot, with per-task paired results and explicit limitations, not statistical proof of superiority.
- Persist partial/failure outcomes. A missing required model blocks that cell and is reported as incomplete rather than silently changing the arm. The report may conclude no advantage or insufficient data. No automatic default-routing change follows this pilot.

## Interface Contract

An explicit benchmark assignment consumes a preregistered manifest of three task contracts/bases, three arms, exact bindings, authorization and acceptance/evaluation rules. It produces per-stage evidence and a comparison report with quality, cost/time and uncertainty columns. Its dry-run/preflight shows nine planned pipeline runs and missing prerequisites without sending prompts or running workloads.

All interfaces described here are proposed acceptance contracts, not a claim of currently installed functionality. Draft usage scenarios are in `ux/usage.md` in this task directory.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] SC1: Preflight a complete manifest and show exactly nine cells; reject changed task/arm definitions after results exist and reject leaked cross-arm inputs.
- [ ] SC2: Complete the pilot or report each blocked/failed cell honestly; every row traces to task, arm, revisions and execution/evaluation evidence.
- [ ] SC3: Reconcile totals including discovery, synthesis, final implementation, reviews, retries and authorized Astra escalation; verify missing values remain unknown.
- [ ] SC4: Blind final evaluation to model names and costs; test that one cheaper failing candidate is not presented as equivalent acceptance.
- [ ] SC5: Produce per-task paired comparisons and a non-prescriptive pilot conclusion; do not promote the sample to a population-level claim or alter default routing.
