# Preserve authoritative receipts through boundary failures — draft usage

## Positive scenario

An accepted protected service remains attributable through contention, restart and ambiguous external outcome without duplicate dispatch or orphan writers.

Reuse the first slice public APIs. No new journal or compatibility protocol. Preserve terminal replay visibility and no-effect settlement contracts.

## Refusal scenario

Wrong peer or stale/missing exact binding returns a classified refusal/uncertainty, invokes no retry and grants no signing authority.

## Acceptance

Race duplicate requests and terminalization with claim; crash after claim, after effect and before/after durable completion; inspect one protected journal chain.
