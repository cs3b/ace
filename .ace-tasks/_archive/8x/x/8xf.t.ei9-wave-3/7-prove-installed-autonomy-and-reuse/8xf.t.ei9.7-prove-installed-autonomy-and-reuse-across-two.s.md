## Failure drills

- Stop worker: status reflects interruption and recovery preserves earlier work.
- Restart overseer during active or waiting attempt: adopt it, no duplicate launch.
- Lose merge/publish/sync response: uncertain until provider proof, no duplicate effect.
- Change PR head after GET: server-side candidate binding refuses unreviewed merge.
- Nil/404 after attempted merge stays uncertain, no false no-effect settlement.
- Deny/revoke/expire decision or mismatch config digest: no effect, exact blocker.
- Omit executor ack/start: deadline reaches actual owner, independent work proceeds.
- Wrong callback target/stale pane/worktree/ref/PR identity: no false ready or overwrite.
- Missing HITL socket/auth, expired or wrong-attempt OTP, orphan request: reconcile safely.
- Source done plus stale consumer/install failure retains installed acceptance open.
