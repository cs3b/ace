# Bound review convergence and expose escalation

## Behavioral Specification

### User Experience

An operator sees why review continues, stops for synthesis, is acceptable, or needs escalation, without endless serial review or premature success.

### Expected Behavior

- Discovery allows at most two completed substantive rounds, then exports the candidate, evidence and uncertainty. An early terminal failure is reported as such. Discovery is never merge-ready.
- Delivery requires at least three completed rounds with the last two free of confirmed High/Critical, and at most five completed rounds before escalation. Earlier unresolved blockers remain blocking. Meeting this search rule alone is not acceptance.
- Required Medium findings must also be repaired and verified. A Medium repair preserves the clean streak but invalidates stale evidence and requires review of affected scope/current-head approval. Optional Low suggestions do not restart the campaign automatically.
- At the five-round boundary, accept only if all delivery requirements are already met; otherwise emit `needs_escalation`. Do not silently increase limits or launch a costly provider. Stricter project acceptance rules remain separate and visible.
- A canonical blocker confirmed unresolved again after its repair/verification attempt triggers diagnosis before another equivalent review. Reopening a closed issue requires new evidence; a new empty report cannot erase it.
- Deduplicate by verified underlying defect while retaining all independent sources. A corrected severity updates the affected round's qualification and derived streak with audit history; it does not invent another round.
- Infrastructure attempts have a separate bound: default two retries after the initial transient failure per provider/scope; authentication, authorization and unavailable-model failures stop that provider immediately. Retries never count as clean reviews.

## Interface Contract

Campaign profile is an explicit input to start/status/finish. Results distinguish `discovery_complete`, `search_converged`, `accepted`, `needs_escalation`, and execution failure with a concrete reason; convergence and evidence validity remain separate fields. Status exposes completed rounds, execution attempts, budget remaining, open findings and permitted next action. No automatic promotion from an exploratory profile to delivery.

All interfaces described here are proposed acceptance contracts, not a claim of currently installed functionality. Draft usage scenarios are in `ux/usage.md` in this task directory.

## Readiness decisions: exact completion and escalation semantics

- **Two separate outputs:** search_converged is derived only from completed qualifying rounds and their verified severity assessments. accepted additionally requires no unresolved required findings, valid current-head/scope independent approval and required passing checks. An unresolved old High blocks acceptance even after two later clean rounds; it does not erase the recorded sequence. Correcting a severity recomputes the affected sequence with an audit record.
- **Required Medium default:** confirmed in-scope Medium findings require repair. Unsupported claims, optional redesigns and true out-of-scope suggestions must be explicitly classified with rationale; relabeling a defect or silently skipping it cannot satisfy acceptance. A requested scope reduction requires an explicit changed contract, not a budget workaround.
- **Round-cap boundary:** count every substantive qualifying report set once, including a necessary final current-head review; no uncounted final review outside the five-round limit. If round five finds a required defect, the campaign needs escalation even if it can be repaired immediately, because new code still requires evidence. If all conditions already hold at round five, accept. A previously executed current-head review may be reused only through existing validated receipt rules, not fabricated from an old-head report.
- **No-op and empty findings:** zero-model no-op is not a round. A genuinely executed, completed review with zero findings can be clean. Retrying the same report/import or splitting it into modules does not create extra clean rounds. Scope expansion requires completion of the added scope, not backfilling a clean verdict by assertion.
- **Discovery:** stop at two completed rounds, with candidate artifact export even when known findings remain; distinguish discovery_complete from accepted. A terminal worker/provider failure before the cap stays failed/incomplete rather than claiming two rounds occurred.
- **Repeated blocker:** a verified recurrence of the same unresolved canonical defect after an attempted repair pauses further equivalent review for diagnosis. Persist defect identity, evidence, attempted fix and recommended next action. Model disagreement alone is not recurrence and automatic text similarity is not proof of duplication.
- **Escalation/resume:** default budget is fixed for the phase. needs_escalation does not launch another model or discard the campaign. An authorized next phase records its reason, selected route and finite additional budget, retaining lifetime counters/findings and the exhausted phase. Without those explicit inputs it remains stopped. Starting again with the same contract does not reset the exhausted budget.
- **Infrastructure budget:** initial call plus at most two retries for each required provider/scope within a round. Failed attempts never count as clean. Authentication/authorization/model-unavailable is terminal for that selection. Missing a required scope keeps the round incomplete; any authorized roster substitution is recorded and cannot silently waive required coverage.
- **Defaults/policy:** discovery cap two and delivery minimum three, clean streak two, cap five are the shipped profiles. A stricter consumer acceptance rule is still required; a conflict with execution limits must be resolved explicitly before execution, never by a hidden sixth call. Budgets limit exploration, not correctness requirements.

### Public status and exit behavior

Reuse R1 campaign operations. JSON exposes phase/profile, total and phase round counts, attempts, remaining budget, search_converged, accepted, open findings, evidence validity and next permitted action. Status is read-only and returns structured blocked/escalation outcomes. Finish accepts discovery export as discovery_complete only; delivery finish is nonzero for needs_escalation or missing requirements. Dry-run evaluates policy without launching models or changing counters; presentation flags do not alter results. No force mode grants acceptance.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] SC1: Discovery exports after two rounds even with known defects and cannot satisfy a merge gate.
- [ ] SC2: High -> clean -> clean meets the search threshold after round three; acceptance additionally requires the earlier defect fixed and current evidence valid. An immediate fix does not retroactively make the High round clean.
- [ ] SC3: A Medium-only sequence can converge while acceptance remains blocked on an unresolved Medium or stale receipt.
- [ ] SC4: Repeated blocker, corrected severity, duplicate findings and an empty later report retain the correct canonical issue and history.
- [ ] SC5: Five incomplete-for-acceptance rounds produce needs_escalation; failed providers consume only bounded attempts; no extra model run is launched without applicable authorization.

- [ ] SC6: Exercise the readiness decisions and public operating modes above through actual CLI/workflow entrypoints: dry-run is non-mutating, JSON is parseable, identity/authority failures cannot become acceptance, and replay or restart preserves the same result.
